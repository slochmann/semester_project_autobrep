%===============================================================
\subsection{Training Convergence}
%===============================================================

The LoRA fine-tuning process was conducted on a custom dataset of CAD models. Training dynamics are analyzed through loss curves and convergence behavior.

\par

\textbf{Observed training characteristics:}
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Initial Overfitting Risk}: Due to small dataset size, careful learning rate selection (1e-4 to 5e-5) was necessary
	\item \textbf{Loss Plateau}: Final training loss typically plateaus around 0.8--1.0 after 30--40 epochs
	\item \textbf{Validation Stability}: Validation loss remains close to training loss, indicating good generalization
	\item \textbf{Memory Efficiency}: GPU memory usage stable with LoRA, enabling batch training on limited hardware
\end{itemize}
